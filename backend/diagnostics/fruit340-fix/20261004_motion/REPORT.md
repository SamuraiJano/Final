# Independent image-motion check for fruit340 / legacy491

Original inputs were left unchanged. All outputs in this directory are diagnostic;
none of these approximate poses is passed to production mapping.

## What the images show

The visible markings are continuous and the fruit changes surface orientation.
This is not a uniformly rolling fruit. There is substantial rotation in the image
plane early in the sequence, much slower motion later, and a change of dominant
motion direction. The camera Z component is explicitly zero in the current
two-component rotation search; the image evidence supports nonzero Z motion.

The contact sheet is `contact_sheet.png`. The large original crop panels and
seven manually annotated freckle images make the observations inspectable.

## Independent spatial feature matches

SIFT and ORB use mutual nearest matches with a0.8 descriptor-ratio threshold,
foreground masks eroded9px, and bright low-saturation highlight exclusion.
The matches are fitted with a bounded three-component rotation using the frozen
spheroid geometry. Reprojection RANSAC uses a2px inlier threshold. RANSAC
matches and fitted rotations are diagnostics, not labelled pose truth.

For308→309 two useful feature variants support the same missing component:

| Variant | Inliers / mutual matches | Spatial span | Vector x,y,z | Median inlier error |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
|Raw green SIFT|7/10|70.5×64.1px|13.08,-8.85,-6.92deg|0.73px|
|High-pass green ORB|20/23|70.1×80.1px|12.30,-8.67,-7.18deg|1.24px|

The freckle was not used as a manual constraint in either fit. Its projection
errors are0.49px and1.37px respectively. Other preprocessing variants agree
approximately with the negative Z component, but have fewer robust inliers.

On all mutual matches, including outliers, the proposed two-axis native rescue
has median errors5.47px(SIFT) and5.34px(ORB), versus1.01px and1.39px for the
three-axis diagnostic fits. Within the three-axis RANSAC inliers, the comparison
is5.08vs0.73px and4.90vs1.24px. The latter comparison favors the model used
for RANSAC selection, so the all-match values are also reported.

This means308→309's rescued two-axis pose may join more views while retaining
systematic texture displacement elsewhere in the image. It should not be
claimed as improved surface accuracy from coverage or the single freckle alone.

Frame309→310 and310→311 do not have enough robust spatial feature inliers
with these settings: no variant exceeds three geometric inliers. Their
automated SIFT/ORB fitted rotations are not trustworthy. The clear freckle
displacement remains useful as a check of any candidate pose at that point.

Data: `feature_motion.json`, `candidate_feature_comparison.json`; render:
`native2axis_vs_feature3d_308_309.png`(blue observed feature; yellow two-axis
prediction; green three-axis prediction).

## Optical-flow cross-check and limits

Centroid-aligned Lucas–Kanade tracks use forward/backward error<0.7px and
exclude a central highlight mask. For the slower identifiable texture pairs,
a sphere approximation with three-component rotation fits better than a
positive-X, zero-Z fit. For example325→326:2.31→0.70px median error;
327→328:2.33→0.71px;328→329:4.48→1.19px. Approximate inferred Z values
are4.54,4.80 and7.99degrees respectively.

LK fails the fast movement308→310, including when initialized only at the
manually labelled source freckle. Its inferred near-zero movement for these
pairs must not be treated as physical truth. Late349→364 motion is much smaller;
subpixel mask-centroid variation and silhouette error can bias its inferred
small reversals. These measurements cannot justify inventing rolling angles
to reach a desired coverage percentage.

Data: `image_motion.json`, `manual_marker_lk.json`.

## Result

There is concrete estimator/geometry-model error in this sequence independent
of tracker IDs: accepted poses miss a visually persistent marker, and spatial
feature matches support a camera Z rotation omitted by the current search.
Dense views with little forward main-axis rotation and large changes in other
components also explain why the configured selector splits this sequence.
An improvement needs measured three-component correspondence and validation
of pose across the fruit surface; relaxing a rejection threshold alone is not
enough evidence of a correct map. No claim of full-surface truth is possible
without measured poses or additional independent surface landmarks.

## Final photometric candidate: independent check

The final native three-component rescue for308→309 is taken from the saved
candidate mapping result: vector(12.523506,-8.604265,-6.606129)degrees.
This vector comes from the image photometric search, independently of the
SIFT/ORB descriptor fits above. Its projection was evaluated against **all**
mutual descriptor matches, without using RANSAC to remove outliers:

| Independent matches | Count | Earlier2D median | Final3D median | Earlier2D mean | Final3D mean | Final3D maximum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
|Raw green SIFT|10|5.470px|1.186px|11.770px|9.666px|75.288px|
|High-pass green ORB|23|5.335px|1.232px|11.172px|7.853px|88.823px|

All six tested feature/preprocessing combinations and all per-point residuals
are preserved in `final_candidate_feature_evidence.json`, alongside the
separate diagnostic3D feature fits and file hashes. No invalid lifted points
were excluded in these two primary comparisons. Very large residuals remain
because automatic descriptor matches include uncertain/wrong correspondences;
the lower medians support the local candidate without establishing full pose
accuracy. The manually marked freckle projects within1.045px, inside its
approximately2px labelling uncertainty.

The saved candidate retains frames305–309 instead of305–308 and estimates
32.220% observed surface instead of26.413%. The map remains partial. This
does not resolve310→311's earlier wrong accepted pose or the discarded later
sequence, and the estimated percentages have no calibrated surface truth.

ORB projections. It is generated by `final_candidate_evidence.py` using the
exact saved candidate vector, and was visually checked for legibility.

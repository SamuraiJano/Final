Independent fruit tracking labels

Give each physical fruit a permanent number. Draw a box around its visible extent on every frame, including fruit at the image edges. Follow the fruit by its appearance and motion. Review all fruit before marking a frame complete. Tracker predictions are hidden.

Drag on empty image to draw a box. Drag an existing box to move it, or its bottom-right corner to resize it. Click New fruit ID to draw inside another box. Copied boxes must be manually corrected. Editing removes that frame's reviewed status. Arrow keys step frames; Delete removes the selected box. Work is saved in this browser; also export a backup.

A complete review with unresolved identity uncertainty cannot certify tracking. This tool measures a fixed video window; separately record which fruit have a complete entry-to-exit passage. A second reviewer should check the labels independently.
